Kalimati supplies 60 to 70 percent of the Kathmandu valley's fruit and vegetables. Its price board decides what a kilo of tomatoes costs in half the city's shops that day.

I built it on my own: the public site, the Android app, the bilingual content, and the admin the market's staff use every morning.

## What's in it

- Daily wholesale prices and arrivals, with history, trends and comparisons
- A minimal Android app for prices, with no ads
- A newsletter that sends only the day's prices
- Trader dues and notices
- A notice centre for tenders, public bills, RTI reports and daily pesticide residue results
- Publications, the citizen charter, and the committee and staff pages

## The admin

Role-based permissions, two-factor sign-in, user impersonation for support, and sortable tables over everything. Staff who aren't technical publish the day's numbers without calling anyone.
