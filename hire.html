Questions

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. I have worked remotely for clients in the United States and in Nepal. Kathmandu is UTC+5:45, which overlaps the working day in Asia, Australia and the Gulf, and European mornings.

Can you take over an existing project?

Yes. I start by reading the code and the data, then write down what is risky and what is fine before changing anything.

Which technologies do you use?

Mostly PHP and Laravel, Vue, Node.js, PostgreSQL and MySQL, on Linux and Docker. WordPress when it is the right tool, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 when the client already runs on it.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes, as a contractor on your team or as the developer behind your client work, under your brand if you prefer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first call you get a fixed quote for a defined project, or a monthly rate for ongoing work.
